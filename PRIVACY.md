# Privacy

dnt (the terminal app and the dnt app for Windows and Android) collects no
personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash
reports, and it sends nothing about you or your games anywhere.

## What stays on your device

Your characters, campaigns, notes, homebrew and settings are files in the
app's own storage (on a computer, the data folder; on Android, the app's
storage). Exports and backups are files written where you choose, or in
the Download/dnt folder on Android. Uninstalling the app deletes its
storage; exports and backups stay where they were written.

## What reaches the internet

dnt connects only to GitHub, and only for:

- **The rules library**, when you open it (Data › Rules library): it
  downloads the library from
  [github.com/v0v3s/dnt-rules](https://github.com/v0v3s/dnt-rules).
- **Updates**, in the builds from this repository's GitHub releases only:
  once a day (you can turn it off in Data › About) or when you ask, dnt
  reads which release is the newest, and downloads it when you update.
  The builds from app stores (Google Play, F-Droid) never do this: the
  store updates them.

These requests carry nothing about you, but GitHub sees them as it sees
any visit, under [GitHub's privacy
statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Permissions on Android

- **Internet**: for the rules library and updates, above.
- **Install apps**: only in the GitHub builds, to hand a downloaded
  update to Android's installer, which asks you first.
